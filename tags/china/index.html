How to Import LV Distribution Panels from China: A Complete Guide for Southeast Asian Buyers

August 15, 2025
Import, China, Guide

Why Import Electrical Panels from China?#

China is the world’s largest manufacturer of low voltage switchgear. For buyers in Southeast Asia, importing from China offers:

  • 30–50% cost savings compared to local OEMs
  • Shorter lead times (30–45 days vs 60–90 days locally)
  • IEC 61439 compliance with full test documentation
  • Flexible customization to your single-line diagram

Step 1: Define Your Technical Requirements#

Before contacting any supplier, prepare the following:

  1. Single-line diagram (or at least a hand-drawn sketch)
  2. Rated voltage (typically 400V or 690V)
  3. Total current rating (main busbar capacity)
  4. Number of outgoing circuits and their ratings
  5. Protection level (IP42 for indoor, IP54/IP65 for outdoor)
  6. Form of separation (Form 2b, 3b, or 4b per IEC 61439)

Step 2: Shortlist and Verify Suppliers#

Red Flags to Avoid:#

  • ❌ No IEC 61439 type test certificate
  • ❌ Refuses to provide factory routine test report
  • ❌ Cannot show previous export cases

What to Verify:#

  • ✅ ISO 9001 certification
  • ✅ IEC 61439 type test report
  • ✅ Previous export cases with photos
  • ✅ Component brands used (Schneider, ABB, Siemens, Chint)

Step 3: Request and Review Quotations#

A proper quotation should include: detailed BOM, single-line diagram, dimension drawings, production lead time, FOB/CIF price, and warranty terms (minimum 12 months).
