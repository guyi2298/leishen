About Us

Company Profile#

Xiangyang Leishen Electric Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer of low voltage switchgear, located in Xiangyang City, Hubei Province, China. Founded in 2010, we have over 15 years of experience in designing and building distribution panels, motor control centers, and custom control cabinets.

What We Do#

We design, manufacture, and test low voltage electrical panels for:

  • 🏭 Industrial plants (textile, cement, steel, food processing)
  • ☀️ Solar & renewable energy projects
  • 💧 Water treatment & pumping stations
  • 🏗️ Commercial buildings & offices
  • 🌴 Palm oil mills

Our Production Process#

  1. Inquiry & Quote — You send requirements, we respond within 24h
  2. Design & Approval — Single-line diagram & layout drawing for your approval
  3. Material Procurement — Copper busbars, breakers, enclosures from certified suppliers
  4. Fabrication — Enclosure welding, busbar bending, component mounting
  5. Wiring & Assembly — Neat wiring per IEC standards
  6. Factory Testing — Insulation resistance, HV test, function test
  7. Packing & Shipping — Wooden crate + desiccant, FOB Shanghai / Ningbo
