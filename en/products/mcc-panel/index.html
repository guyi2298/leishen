Motor Control Center (MCC)

Motor Control Center (MCC)#

Our MCC panels provide centralized motor control and protection for industrial applications. Designed with modular drawers, each motor circuit is independently removable for maintenance without shutting down the entire system.

Key Specifications#

ParameterValue
Rated Voltage400V / 690V AC
Motor Power Range0.75kW – 355kW
Control TypeDOL / Star-Delta / VFD / Soft Starter
Protection LevelIP42 / IP54
StandardsIEC 61439-1, IEC 60947
Withdrawable DrawersYes (fixed + plug-in options)

Features#

  • ✅ Modular drawer design (fixed, plug-in, withdrawable)
  • ✅ VFD and soft starter integration
  • ✅ PLC/DCS communication (Modbus RTU/TCP, Profibus)
  • ✅ Motor protection relay with fault diagnosis
  • ✅ Emergency stop and interlocking functions

Applications#

  • Textile factories
  • Cement plants
  • Water treatment plants
  • Palm oil mills
  • Food & beverage processing
  • Chemical plants
