Power Factor Correction Panel

Power Factor Correction (PFC) Panel#

Our automatic PFC panels help industrial facilities reduce reactive power penalties and improve energy efficiency. Capable of maintaining power factor above 0.99.

Key Specifications#

ParameterValue
Rated Voltage400V / 690V AC
Capacitor Bank25 kVar – 1000 kVar
Switching MethodContactor / Thyristor
Target Power Factor0.99
Protection LevelIP40 / IP54
StandardsIEC 60831, IEC 61439

Features#

  • ✅ Automatic switching based on real-time load
  • ✅ Thyristor-switched for fast response
  • ✅ Harmonic filter option (7th harmonic)
  • ✅ LCD display with power factor readout
  • ✅ Over-temperature and over-voltage protection

Applications#

  • Factories with inductive loads (motors, transformers)
  • Commercial buildings with central HVAC
  • Data centers
  • Steel mills & foundries
